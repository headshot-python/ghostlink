# Product — Ghostlink

Ghostlink resurrects dead links. Paste a URL that no longer loads and get the
page back from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine.

Who it is for: anyone who follows old links — readers, bloggers fixing link rot,
researchers citing old pages, developers maintaining old docs.

What it is not: a proxy, a scraper, or an archive. Ghostlink only asks the
Wayback Machine what it already saved, and shows it. It is not affiliated with
the Internet Archive.

Tone: playful-haunted, never gross. Tombstones, ghosts, "resurrection" — the
Halloween theme is the joke; the tool itself is serious and fast.
